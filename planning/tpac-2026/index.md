# CSSWG meeting at TPAC 2026

[TPAC 2026](https://www.w3.org/news-events/tpac/2026/) is scheduled for **26-30 October 2026** in Dublin, Ireland with options for both in-person and remote participation. 

- CSSWG F2F: **Thursday-Friday, 29-30 October**
- [high level TPAC schedule](https://www.w3.org/news-events/tpac/2026/schedule/)
- [full TPAC schedule](https://www.w3.org/calendar/tpac2026/)

## Covid Protocols

- Masks recommended in the meeting room Thursday and Friday
- COVID testing before arrival, and daily before meetings
- Outdoor dining encouraged, weather permitting
- Strong recommendation to wear a mask during your entire travel journey (onboard a flight, train or federally-regulated vessel, as well as in airports and other points of entry) except for brief periods while eating, drinking, or taking oral medication, or unless otherwise exempt
